## 4. Sharing

We share personal data only with:

- **suppliers** that help run the Service (hosting, e-mail delivery, object storage, payment), under contracts that oblige them to protect it;
- **authorities**, when the law or a court order requires it;
- a **successor** in a merger or acquisition, under this same policy.

We do not sell personal data.

## 5. International transfers

Data is hosted in {{ data_location }}. When a transfer abroad happens, we rely on the mechanisms of LGPD art. 33 and GDPR chapter V, such as standard contractual clauses.

## 6. Retention

We keep personal data while your account is active and, after it is closed, only for as long as needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. Security logs are kept for a limited period and then deleted. Workspace content can be exported for 30 days after the workspace is closed, and is then deleted from the Service and, at the end of the backup cycle, from backups.

## 7. Security

We use measures such as encryption in transit and at rest, isolation between workspaces, mandatory second-factor authentication, least-privilege access, logs and backups. No system is risk-free: if an incident is likely to cause relevant harm, we notify the authority and the people affected, as the law requires.

## 8. Your rights

Under the LGPD and the GDPR you may ask for: confirmation that we process your data; access; correction; anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data; portability; information about sharing; withdrawal of consent; and review of automated decisions. Under the GDPR you may also object to processing and ask for its restriction.

Send requests to {{ dpo_email }}. We may need to confirm your identity. If the data belongs to a customer's workspace, we forward the request to that customer. You may also complain to the data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

## 9. Cookies

The API uses no advertising cookies. Strictly necessary cookies or local storage may be used to keep you signed in and remember your language.

## 10. Children

The Service is not directed to children. Customers who process data of children or adolescents in their workspace must follow the specific legal rules.
