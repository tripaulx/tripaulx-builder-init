# Incident register

> **Template.** This page sets the policy. Keep each record in a controlled place (a restricted repository or document) based on the incident record template.

**Owner:** {{ dpo_name }} · **Last review:** {{ effective_date }}

## 1. Policy

- **Every** security incident is recorded, including the ones that did not require notification. The register is the evidence that {{ company }} assessed each event.
- The record is opened on detection, not at the end, and is updated during the response.
- Records are kept for at least **5 years**.
- Access is limited to the incident lead, the DPO and management.
- Records hold the minimum personal data needed. Refer to people by role or by an internal identifier whenever possible.

## 2. Identifier

Use `INC-YYYY-NNN`, for example `INC-2025-001`, sequential within the year.

## 3. Minimum content

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| ID | `INC-YYYY-NNN` |
| Detected at | Date and time (with time zone) and by whom |
| Severity | Critical, High, Medium or Low (see the response plan) |
| Description | What happened, in plain words |
| Data and people affected | Categories and approximate number |
| Systems | Components and suppliers involved |
| Timeline | Main facts with date and time |
| Containment and recovery | Actions taken and when |
| Risk assessment | Likelihood of harm to people and why |
| Notifications | Authority, people and customers: date, or reason for not notifying |
| Root cause | Established after the investigation |
| Lessons and actions | Links to the risk matrix and the backlog |
| Closed at | Date and who approved the closing |
